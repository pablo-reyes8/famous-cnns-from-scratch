# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community regardless of experience, identity, background, disability, appearance, nationality, or beliefs.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Credit prior work and respect privacy.
- Accept feedback and correct mistakes in good faith.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer at `alejogranados229@gmail.com`. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and fairly. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
